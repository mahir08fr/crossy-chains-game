The GIF is a lightweight low-frame-rate capture, not a 60 FPS video or a frame-rate benchmark. The controlled motion capture had a scheduling gap; the included clip uses the subsequent consecutive frames, with the team settled and traffic still moving. No frames were synthesized or interpolated. A higher-frame-rate traversal video and a later dense-world overview remain useful future additions.

These local captures show implemented gameplay and presentation. They do not establish real four-person coordination, phone compatibility, public-hosting capacity or endurance acceptance. Invitation codes, diagnostic panels, browser chrome and personal information are excluded from the published crops.
